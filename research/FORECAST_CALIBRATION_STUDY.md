# Same information, revised source, unchanged forecasts

**Supplementary finite calibration · 24 October 2026 · accompanying book v3.6.0**

## Finding

An actual model-authored forecasting program already handled appended evidence.
Giving that unchanged program the new facts reproduced every tested forecast of
eight subsequent revision attempts. Additional organization of the same records
produced no measured predictive increment in this fixed packet.

All nine model calls, 133 source captures and 146 scored truth rows completed.
There were no missing outcomes, invalid forecasts, replacements or retries.
The 146 rows comprise 144 evaluation rows and two comparisons against a single
shared diagnostic execution; they are not 146 independent executions.

| Mean transfer Brier, lower is better | World u0 | World u1 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Original M0, initial evidence E0 | 3/32 | 11/32 |
| Same unchanged M0, appended evidence E1 | 1/16 | 3/16 |
| Source returned after feedback, flat records, each repeat | 1/16 | 3/16 |
| Source returned after feedback, organized records, each repeat | 1/16 | 3/16 |

Six of the eight returned source bodies are byte-identical to M0; only two
differ. A revision attempt was permitted to retain the source. Byte changes alone
do not establish a changed method.

Registered flat-minus-organized transfer contrast D=0 in each world and overall.
Every two-repeat within-format range is zero. The **96 revised distributions**
equal the corresponding unchanged M0(E1) distribution, beyond merely equal means.
All 32 revised retention rows have zero loss, with no measured own-source
retention regression relative to actual M0. The unchanged controls also retain
their four predictions per world with zero loss.

## Question and design

This developer-authored, AI-assisted calibration tests presentation during
**source authoring**, with identical canonical runtime evidence and query bytes.
It is not an agent-memory product benchmark or a book-reading intervention.

The declared implementation class is a two-level cache with 64 configurations.
Two configurations and 16 episode templates were fixed before target inference:
two initial episodes, two feedback episodes, eight transfer compositions and four
compatible retention episodes. The configurations are nonrandom, dependent
authored worlds; they were withheld from generation/runtime inputs and are
disclosed in the selected case data after the cohort.

1. One actual shared M0 was generated from E0. It was retained without screening
   for failure, replacing a correct source or requiring a new AST.
2. That source executed once on exposed diagnostic operations before their new
   outcomes were supplied. Its valid distribution was 1/2–1/2, with realized
   Brier 1/2 in each world. This supplied information, not proof of a broken method.
3. Eight fresh generation attempts received that same actual source/note/capture
   and the appropriate world's E1 ledger: two worlds × two formats × two repeats,
   in a fixed interleaved order. All branches could retain, reorganize or rebuild.
4. The flat chronological view and organized grouped/indexed view recover the
   same literal records. A common legend explains both. Neither provides a
   posterior, recommended repair, future-query answer or extra action right.
5. All eight generations finished before evaluation on e05–e16. The same frozen
   M0 was also evaluated under E0 and E1. Comparable E1 runtime packets are
   byte-identical across sources, irrespective of the authoring presentation.

Evaluation required 12 shared M0(E0) captures scored against both worlds,
24 M0(E1) captures and 96 revised-source captures. With the one diagnostic this
is 133 actual captures. Transfer and retention are kept separate; both repeats
and both worlds remain in the result.

## Endpoint and missingness

Programs print a closed categorical distribution over every permitted write
origin. Omitted labels have zero probability. Probabilities are exact rational
strings with exact unit mass; duplicate keys, unknown labels and malformed
tokens are refused rather than repaired. Realized categorical Brier ranges 0–2.

The registered primary was a composite: valid Brier, or a separately labeled
loss 2 for a bound malformed source carrier or clean exit 0 invalid forecast.
Nonzero exit, host/observer failure, uncertain binding or unknown main usage
would stop the cohort with primary null, never an attributed participant loss.
No such failure occurred; all observed primary values are valid Brier here.

For each world and format, each repeat's L is its eight-query transfer mean.
D is the mean of the two flat L values minus the mean of the two organized L
values; overall D averages both worlds. Retention has four separate queries.
Two repeats do not support a significance test or a reliable variance estimate.

Organized used 2,488 additional input tokens, **3.3551% relative to flat**, with
equal tested forecasts. These are observed finite-call costs, not a general
causal price estimate. Caching and wall time differed; no billed-currency or
energy comparison was measured.

The common M0 cost is separate: 15,894 input, 1,687 output and 11,776 cached.
All nine calls total **166,694 input / 12,003 output / 61,824 cached subset**.
Cache is included in input, never added to it. Main and cached usage are known
for all nine calls. Summed native-observer time is 250.511765 s; summed source
capture elapsed is 8.118117 s. These distinct scopes are not calendar duration or
full engineering/team inference cost.

The requested route was GPT-6.1 Sol/high through fresh generation calls, with
tools/project documents/hooks/memory/apps disabled. This is requested/static
lineage, not resolved provider/model attestation. Model observation had a 240 s
wall and1 MiB combined stream bound; source execution had 3 s wall,2 s CPU,256 MiB
address-space and64 KiB output bounds. Cumulative reported input >250,000 or
output >50,000 would stop later stages; these post-call checks were not a hard
native token or currency ceiling. No stopping threshold was reached.

## What this identifies

On these tested inputs, unchanged information conditioning sufficed. Neither
source revision nor added organization improved the registered predictive
outcome. Correct initial sources and the simpler account remain part of the
finding. Nonzero residual realized loss need not be an epistemic error under
the uncertainty actually available to the participant.

The initial M0 already implemented an enumerator/filter over the declared class.
This material did not establish necessary method repair, discovery of an unknown
law, formation or model-weight learning. Equal tested vectors do not establish
identical procedures on untested inputs or absence of internal record use.
This single authored family and two-repeat design do not establish universal
format equivalence or refute the broader ontology. Book v3.6.0 and claim statuses,
including C03 unsupported, remain unchanged; C03 concerns a different intervention.

## Verification and publication limits

The local protocol freeze preceded target calls; it was not independently
registered or authenticated. A separate local saved-data implementation checked
the complete private records without rerunning candidates. The public verifier
regrades the **selected** saved task/prompt/source/request/output data, score
weights, forecast equality, retention and costs. It does not regenerate model
responses, execute archived Python or simulate the cache to establish truth.
Selected saved authored actual labels are its ground-truth inputs.

The mapping lists original digests and omissions. Full native transport,
operational identifiers, environment, original controller/host tree and private
working context are omitted. A digest of an excluded container does not expose
or authenticate its selected fields. Public regrading does not prove full-tree
equality, historical order, independent replication, provider identity, billing
or whole-process-group closure. Coherent replacement of both evidence and the
trusted verifier lies outside the integrity claim.

Source strings are inert untrusted data. A changed source, changed forecast,
beneficial outcome and recorded return path remain separate claims.
