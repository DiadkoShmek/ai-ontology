# Unrelated reading control

`UNRELATED_READING_SOURCE_UA.txt` and its literal prefix
`UNRELATED_READING_UA.txt` contain Ukrainian paragraphs from Ivan Franko's
**Захар Беркут** (original literary work, 1883), transcribed on Ukrainian
Wikisource from the 1960 collected-works edition, chapters I–IV.

Source: [Ukrainian Wikisource](https://uk.wikisource.org/wiki/Твори_(Франко,_1956–1962)/13/Захар_Беркут).
Per-chapter URLs, fetched-page and extracted-text hashes are retained in
`unrelated-reading-provenance.json`. Navigation, executable content, tables and
footnote bodies were removed. The matched control is a literal prefix, not a
newly authored or paraphrased version.

Credit: **Ivan Franko; Ukrainian Wikisource contributors.** The original literary
work is public domain. The source site's transcription attribution and
[CC BY-SA 4.0 terms](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) are retained
for these separate study-control files. DiadkoShmek claims no authorship of
this material. It is not incorporated into the ontology book or its PDFs.

This is unrelated literary reading, not an assumed inert intervention. It
shares a broad language with the book but differs in genre, vocabulary, age
and themes. Matching native input length does not isolate all those features.
Those differences limit interpretation of that comparison.
