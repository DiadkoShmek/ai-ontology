# Reading-mediated transfer through an authored operating approach

**Main protocol, frozen before held-out task inference.**
Source bytes, actual reading, schedule, scorer and analysis are pinned in the
companion freeze. Development calibration V3 selected a 3200-byte channel from
ordinary-profile outcomes only. Earlier qualifications remain separate.

## Question and conditions

Does prior reading change an agent's proposed operating approach in a way that
improves later transfer of an unseen engineering task through a bounded carry?
This tests a specific chain of artifacts, not subjective experience or a general
effect of the entire book. Textual policy adaptation is not model-weight learning.

Four conditions share an ordinary participant profile and the same induction
question: propose a justified public working approach before seeing the task.
Adoption, adaptation and rejection of supplied reading are all permitted.

- Ordinary profile, no additional reading.
- Book reading: ten conceptual/engineering parts, about 16 269 whitespace words.
  The empirical chapter, research programme, opening and references are excluded.
- Operational guide: the actual short implementation-method chapter, which
  conveys the same practical ideas without the extended exposition.
- Unrelated Ukrainian literary reading, selected as a literal source prefix
  to match the book condition's native input-token load within 1 percent.

The reading control is not assumed inert or genre-matched. The book includes
discussion of the same reference runtime; any observed advantage may include
rehearsal or practical instruction. The guide contrast helps examine the value
of extended exposition but does not isolate every conceptual or stylistic
ingredient. No condition receives additional source-project facts.

## Competing explanations

1. The book-derived approach improves retained relations and downstream useful
   planning compared with an ordinary prepared profile.
2. A short operational guide is sufficient; extended exposition adds no
   measured advantage over that guide.
3. Reading adds no useful transfer, or increases errors, deferrals or cost.
   Neither extra caution nor an unmeasured inner benefit rescues a failed task.

Native input-length preparation found 39 975 reading tokens for the book and
39 932 for the selected unrelated prefix after subtracting the common wrapper:
about 0.108 percent difference. Four unscored data-length probes are retained;
none generated a participant approach or received work cases or gold. Exact
provider billing and full isolation of genre/style are not established.

## Three fresh contexts per episode

1. **Preparation:** reading and common role only. Return a public approach,
   without source history, future task, gold or parent conversation.
2. **Application:** a fresh sender receives the actual approach plus the source
   journal, static inventory and reference code. Write one whole carry note.
   It cannot see the future withdrawal or budget. The original reading is not
   copied into this context. No correct approach or note is substituted.
3. **Transfer:** a common fresh receiver gets only the actual stored note, the
   common inventory/reference, the new operation and work budget. It sees no
   condition, original history, reading or approach. Truncation is explicit.

The storage adapter uses the fixed calibrated UTF-8 prefix capacity. Whole notes
have a generous 12000-byte cap; no silently rewritten, best-of or corrected note
is permitted. Only a safe proposed plan is admitted to the temporary artifact;
its bytes are read back. No generated code or real release operation executes.

## Cases and sample

Use the eight held-out instances sealed in transfer calibration V3, previously
unseen by task inference: four source seeds, two counterfactual histories each.
They form one finite planning class, not eight independent problem families.
Each history pair has the same initial statuses and entities but one different
basis edge; after withdrawal their optimal plans differ. The model must reason
over branch/scope, revisions, transitive support, current objective, budget and
work prerequisites. Gold is obtained from the unchanged runtime and two subset
solvers. No ready post-withdrawal eligibility table is supplied.

Run two fresh repetitions for every case/condition: 16 episodes per condition,
64 episodes total and at most 192 participant calls. Add one common full-history
receiver per held-out case: at most 8 control calls. All errors, invalid outputs,
missing replies and bounded-stage failures stay in the episode denominator.
Do not replace failed calls. Stop at this registered coverage, not a desired
effect. Native CLI transport retries are not controlled by the evaluator.

All cases and gold predate the development calibration. The difficulty setting
is selected from ordinary-profile development only; no book-condition task
response may influence it. If V3 does not qualify, the main run stays unstarted.
Prior calibration is developer-controlled and exploratory, not independent
external preregistration or peer review.

## Outcomes and analysis

Primary: admitted optimal-value plan with minimum cost among value ties.
Secondary: safety, exact source traceability, certified optimal completion,
normalized admitted utility, empty plans, malformed approaches/notes, target
errors, truncation, native input/output/cache counts and wall time. A courteous
reason or refusal does not receive automatic practical credit. Reasons and
approaches are archived, not scored as natural voice or philosophical depth.

Primary contrast: book minus ordinary profile, paired by source and repetition.
Secondary contrasts: book minus operational guide and unrelated reading. Report
every episode and paired win/loss/tie table, not only aggregate scores. Aggregate
paired differences by the four source seeds before uncertainty calculations;
counterfactual variants and repetitions are dependent. Enumerate the 256 ordered
four-cluster bootstrap resamples for a descriptive percentile interval. Four
clusters give weak precision; no population-wide significance, universal effect,
equivalence or historical novelty is established by these estimates.

The exact interpretation follows the observed outcome. A null result is not
silently turned into evidence of a hidden boost, and a local gain is not a
demonstration of generalized agency. The model route, common harness, source
generator, repeated reading and admission adapter remain part of the estimand.
