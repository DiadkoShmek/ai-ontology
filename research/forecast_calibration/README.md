  final source carriers/prompts,133 source/request/output records and selected
  terminal/cost fields. Synthetic configurations are disclosed after the cohort,
  outside generation/runtime inputs.

- `verify_projection.py`: bounded strict JSON/gzip and independent Fraction
  regrading, with embedded publication pins. Source strings remain inert data.
- `test_projection.py`: focused semantic/corruption counterexamples, including
  coherent inner rehashing and an inert-source execution trap.

This is a selected evidence packet, not the complete original native/controller
tree. Whole excluded-container digests are declarations; their selected fields
cannot be authenticated from a digest alone. Local protocol freeze is not
independent preregistration or proof of historical order/provider identity.
Regrading does not regenerate responses or establish whole-process-group closure,
currency/energy/full engineering cost. Coherent replacement of evidence and its
trusted verifier is outside the integrity claim.

The result identifies zero incremental forecast benefit for these authoring
formats/revisions on this finite packet. It is not necessary method repair,
formation, universal format equivalence or a refutation of the broader ontology.
