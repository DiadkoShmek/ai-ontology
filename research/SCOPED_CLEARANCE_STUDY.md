# Executable revision after a scoped release-contract change

**Supplementary report · 23 October 2026 · [Українська версія](SCOPED_CLEARANCE_STUDY.uk.md)**

## Abstract

We examined whether explicit organization of basis, scope, assessment and return improves executable procedure revision. Eight model-generated controllers were forked into four revision conditions and compared with fresh construction. Every revision condition and fresh construction received an explicit new contract. The original run captured all 49 generation calls but was interrupted during execution; its registered primary remains null. A separately frozen, zero-inference reproduction completed 960 executions of the unchanged controllers. Every revision condition and fresh construction achieved 160/160 correct artifacts; initial controllers achieved 120/160 under the new-contract counterfactual. No incremental scaffold benefit or achieved-score advantage from diagnostic feedback was observed. These are descriptive results for one authored family.

## 1. Task and observable outcome

The workflow releases requested fields and repairs earlier deliveries. Directed `quote` and `derive` edges propagate private-material restrictions; `inspiration` does not. Under OLD, a private node or a content path reaching one prevents release.

The NEW clause permits a clearance to cut that propagation only when it matches the exact node, version, SHA-256 of its UTF-8 value, project, recipient, purpose and epoch. Revoked tokens or epochs invalidate the exception; another uncleared private path still blocks release. Cases include cycles, alternate parents, longer paths, changed material, stale grants and scope mismatches. These are stipulated permissions in a fictional domain, not authenticated real-world authority.

Previously delivered forbidden outputs in the current context require withdrawal by exact delivery ID. Queued, foreign-context and allowed rows must remain unchanged. Notification requires contact consent; without it, withdrawal remains required and the notification duty stays unresolved. Local tombstones retain history and cannot erase recipient knowledge. Fixtures stipulate that prior deliveries refer to the same immutable material snapshot.

A common host executed submitted plans through actual local SQLite writes without repairing unsafe proposals. After reopening each database, the grader checked released values, retained deliveries, historical rows, withdrawals, notices, unresolved duties and manifest binding. Binary success required the complete warranted artifact, including useful releases: blanket withholding could not pass. The permission oracle used matrix closure; an authored generic control used a graph walker. Repair grading independently checked reopened rows.

## 2. Generation and comparison

Eight ephemeral M0 calls received the OLD contract and interface. Each controller then ran on one initial manifest. All eight artifacts met OLD requirements; none met the later NEW counterfactual. The contract change does not retroactively invalidate the earlier action.

Each M0 source and initial manifest were forked into:

| Condition | Diagnostic artifact and assessment | Additional scaffold |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary actual | Supplied | None |
| Linked actual | Supplied | 108 words |
| Ordinary masked | Withheld | None |
| Linked masked | Withheld | 108 words |

Every revision arm received the same NEW clause and competence questions. Masking removed diagnostic consequences, not the requirement. The linked scaffold requested organization around basis, conditions, obligations and return, without an answer or prescribed representation. Ordinary revision could organize freely. Both returned Python source under the same 32,768-byte limit and revision rights. Fresh construction received NEW plus the initial manifest, without M0 source or history: a different-information comparator.

The requested route was GPT-6.1 Sol with high reasoning effort, through an isolated CLI without tools or parent conversation; this is not independent provider-model attestation. There were 48 scored generation calls plus one canary, no outcome-based retries or replacements. Sources remained fixed during transfer; no transfer feedback returned to the model.

Each of the 48 sources faced the same 20 planned worlds for its unit. Thus each arm has 160 executions across eight generation clusters, not 160 independent model generations. Worlds varied identifiers, ordering, material combinations, permission exceptions and retained duties. A step had 3-second wall and 2-second CPU limits; an episode allowed six steps and cost 20. Confinement and virtual-address limits are bounded execution controls, not general security certification or aggregate memory guarantees.

## 3. Interruption and separate reproduction

The original local source freeze preceded inference. All 49 generation outputs were captured, but the process exited during replay (exit130; the source of interruption is unknown). Of 960 planned executions, 620 had bound records, six had SQLite files without complete binding records, and 334 were missing. The original cohort remains interrupted/partial and its registered paired primary remains **null**.

After interruption, a new local plan pinned the original evidence, generated sources, runtime and observer. It executed all 960 episodes into fresh stores, with zero new model calls, no initial-artifact reruns and no selection or replacement. This separate phase completed on 23 October 2026 in **188.001 seconds**. All 620 bound overlaps agreed in both artifact digest and score. This agreement does not establish what happened in the six unbound or 334 missing original episodes. Supplementary descriptive analysis was outside the original freeze but pinned before reproduction execution. Neither local freeze constitutes independent preregistration.

## 4. Results

| Frozen controller set | NEW-correct artifacts | Repairs versus M0 | Regressions versus M0 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| M0 | 120/160 | — | — |
| Ordinary actual | 160/160 | 40 | 0 |
| Linked actual | 160/160 | 40 | 0 |
| Ordinary masked | 160/160 | 40 | 0 |
| Linked masked | 160/160 | 40 | 0 |
| Fresh | 160/160 | 40 | 0 |

M0 remained OLD-correct in 160/160 cases. Each revised set changed 40 artifact plans and retained success on the other 120. Each retained all 320 OLD-permitted field opportunities. Prior-delivery repair was already correct in M0 on all 16 applicable cases per arm; it is not an M1 gain. No unsafe new release, denied-contact attempt or missing required unresolved duty was recorded. Authored controls scored generic 160/160, OLD-content 120/160, all-release 40/160 and withhold 0/160.

Every unit-level linked-minus-ordinary actual difference was zero. Actual-minus-masked and revision-minus-fresh differences were also zero. Ties do not establish equivalence. Diagnostic feedback was unnecessary to the achieved score here; whether a controller internally used it is unobserved. The generic control and masked results show that the explicit clause was sufficient for successful solutions in this family.

## 5. Resources, evidence and limits

The original [49-call resource record](scoped_clearance/costs.json) reports **809,931 input tokens**, including **350,336 cached input tokens**, and **63,408 output tokens**. Cache counts are a subset of input, not additional tokens; all calls had usage reports. Summed call wall time was **1,848.516 seconds**, not elapsed cohort time. Each reproduced arm used 320 steps and cost 800: two steps and cost five per episode. These are stipulated tool-cost units, not monetary costs.

The [archive](scoped_clearance/projection.json.gz) and [offline verifier](scoped_clearance/verify_projection.py) expose prompts, responses, generated sources, selected technical source and SQLite evidence with hash bindings. Verification reopens artifacts and recomputes outcomes without inference or controller execution. This projection omits original partial transfer artifacts and native identities/error details; it establishes neither full-tree equality nor authenticated provenance.

The study demonstrates revision of executed local procedures under a supplied clause, not discovery of that clause. Ceiling performance limits sensitivity to organizational benefits. Authored clustered cases, one requested route and a finite exact oracle restrict generalization. Source edits do not prove new algorithm formation, and local effects do not establish production reliability. No conclusion about the book’s overall benefit, voice, subjectivity or necessity of ontology follows. All revision routes accommodated the changed scope and preserved tested obligations, without measured scaffold benefit.
